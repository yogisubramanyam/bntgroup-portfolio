From machines to software.

An engineer's path — mechanical, then robotics, then the web.

My story

Engineering has always been the constant

I started in Mechanical Engineering for my B.Tech, where I learned how physical systems are designed, analyzed, and built. Even then I was drawn to teaching — I tutored students part-time throughout my undergraduate years, which taught me how to break complex problems into pieces anyone can understand.

My M.Tech in Robotics Engineering brought hardware and software together — control systems, automation, and programming machines to sense and act. That's where software went from a tool to a passion.

I then spent two years as a frontend engineer, building production user interfaces and learning what it takes to ship software that real users depend on every day.

Today I work as a freelance full-stack developer, delivering complete MERN and MEAN applications for clients — from data model and API design through polished, responsive frontends and deployment.

What drives me

The same thing that pulled me from mechanics to robotics to the web: taking an idea, engineering it carefully, and watching it work in the real world. A good web application is a machine too — every part has a purpose.
