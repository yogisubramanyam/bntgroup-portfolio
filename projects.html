HRMS Platform — Full Rebuild

One system for the entire employee lifecycle — records, attendance, leave, and approvals.

The problem

The client's existing HR system was aging and hard to maintain — HR staff were working around it with spreadsheets, and managers had no clear view of attendance or pending leave requests.

What I built

A ground-up rebuild on the MEAN stack. I redesigned the data model, rebuilt every module, and gave each role — HR admin, manager, employee — its own dashboard showing exactly what it needs, secured with role-based access control.

Role: sole full-stack developer — from data model to deployment.

Key features

  • Employee directory with profiles and documents
  • Attendance tracking with daily and monthly views
  • Leave requests with multi-step approval workflows
  • Separate dashboards per role (HR / manager / employee)
  • Reports for headcount, attendance, and leave balances

ATS — Applicant Tracking System

Every candidate, every stage, one pipeline — from application to offer.

  • Interview rounds with per-stage feedback
  • Status tracking so no applicant falls through

Driving School App

Bookings, instructors, and student progress — without the phone calls and paper registers.

The problem

The school ran on phone bookings and paper records — double-booked instructors, no reliable view of a student's progress, and hours of admin every week.

What I built

An operations app tailored to how a driving school actually works: lessons are booked against real instructor availability, every student has a profile with lesson history and progress, and the admin panel manages instructors, slots, and schedules in one place.

Role: sole full-stack developer — built and shipped end to end.

Key features

  • Lesson booking against instructor availability
  • Student enrollment, profiles, and lesson history
  • Progress tracking across the course
  • Admin panel for instructors, slots, and schedules
  • Clear daily/weekly schedule views for staff

Project Management App

Projects, tasks, and deadlines — with a manager's overview and a member's focus.

The problem

  • Personal "my tasks" view for team members
